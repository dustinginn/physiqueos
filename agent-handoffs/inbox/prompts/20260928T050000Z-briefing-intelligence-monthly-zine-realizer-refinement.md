FOUNDER PRODUCT INTENT FOR MONTHLY

Monthly is intentionally different in editorial depth.

It should feel like a monthly physique review / zine: a rich, polished breakdown of the previous month where the user can really understand what changed, what progressed, what did not, how the major evidence domains relate to the Goal, what the strategy means now, and what matters in the coming month.

A month is long enough that meaningful progress and multiple developments should often exist. The Monthly should not be afraid to be wordy where the evidence supports it.

This does NOT mean:
- data dumping;
- repeating four Weekly briefings;
- forcing every domain into prose;
- padding a quiet month;
- abandoning the shared Briefing Intelligence engine.

It means the Monthly information budget and editorial structure should provide substantially more room than Weekly for multi-week synthesis.

MONTHLY EDITORIAL CONTRACT

Using the shared evidence picture and holistic synthesis, define/refine a Monthly-specific editorial contract that can support a zine-like review.

The exact section names should respect existing product presentation where possible, but semantically the Monthly should have room for:

1. Opening / hero:
- concise editorial headline;
- richer summary of what defined the month and how it relates to the Goal.

2. Progress / trajectory:
- body composition where available;
- weight trajectory where relevant;
- guardrail;
- what changed across the month.

3. Training:
- meaningful performance progression;
- PRs/milestones;
- frequency/consistency/persistence;
- not a list of every workout.

4. Nutrition / energy:
- meaningful monthly pattern;
- target relationship;
- consistency/distribution/reliability;
- wearable energy treated with humility.

5. Activity / execution / routine:
- meaningful persistent patterns versus one-offs;
- do not repeat training/nutrition copy.

6. Other goal-relevant domains:
- Photos/visual evidence when available;
- Recovery/Sleep when available in the future;
- other evidence only when it earns space.

7. Strategy / Coach synthesis:
- what the combined month means;
- what should continue/change;
- what is still uncertain;
- no causal overclaiming.

8. Coming month:
- priorities;
- next meaningful evidence/checkpoint;
- what to watch.

This is not a mandate that every Monthly always renders eight prose sections. The shared engine and Monthly policy should decide which sections have enough material. But the product must have the editorial capacity for a rich month.
