# Accessibility contract

- Preserve 44 pt minimum targets for back, Close, Read Photo Briefing, Open gallery, history records, Show All/Close, PDF, pose pager, media retry and inspector Close.
- Latest and history thumbnails announce date/set context, view count and comparison availability; decorative placeholder geometry is hidden from accessibility.
- Paired photo detail announces pose, Previous/Current role and both dates. Each image is independently tappable and identifies its inspector start position.
- Disabled pose-edge controls remain distinguishable without relying on opacity alone in the implementation.
- Source History disclosure exposes expanded/collapsed value and keeps its server-owned text in reading order.
- Full-screen inspection exposes Close, pose/role, date and count. Pinch/double-tap hints remain available; accessibility escape dismisses.
- DEXA charts announce series title, scan count, selected date and formatted value/unit. Tap/drag is not the only path to the current value.
- Every DEXA disclosure exposes expanded/collapsed value. Expanded rows precede the graphs they describe.
- Dark/mineral-light semantic colors are paired with explicit labels and sufficient foreground contrast.
- Dynamic Type may stack the Photos hero, paired images, metrics or pager without changing section order, roles or actions.
