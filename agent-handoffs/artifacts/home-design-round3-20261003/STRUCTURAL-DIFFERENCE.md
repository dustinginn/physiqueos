# Lane B structural-difference test

Reference for rejection: A1 Preferred Dark. Color is removed in [the grayscale comparison](screens/structural-grayscale-test.png) so the test evaluates composition rather than palette.

## B1 Modular Flight Deck — PASS

Changes five dimensions:

- composition: asymmetric two-column module matrix instead of a vertical stack;
- geometry: tall trajectory module paired with confidence/action modules;
- grouping: Goal metrics and phase progression split into peer modules;
- density: three priorities form a horizontal execution rail;
- visual flow: scanning moves across columns before proceeding downward.

Its grayscale rectangles cannot be overlaid onto A1.

## B2 Editorial Spine — PASS

Changes five dimensions:

- surface model: open canvas; cards are reserved for actions;
- hierarchy: trajectory/confidence becomes a typographic masthead;
- geometry: paired action blocks and a vertical phase spine;
- grouping: Goal/phase/guardrail becomes one editorial progression;
- visual flow: rules, alignment and the spine replace nested containers.

Almost none of A1's major card rectangles remain.

## B3 Execution Deck — PASS

Changes five dimensions:

- hierarchy/order: execution precedes trajectory and Goal context;
- grouping: Morning Weight, Briefing and every priority share one dominant deck;
- surface use: trajectory becomes an edge-to-edge persistent ribbon;
- geometry: Goal phases become paired lanes;
- spatial relationship: the Goal is subordinate context rather than the top-half anchor.

The grayscale scan path begins with “Now,” unlike A1's trajectory-led stack.

## B4 Immersive Trajectory Map — PASS

Changes five dimensions:

- composition: one continuous trajectory environment replaces hero plus Goal stack;
- geometry: confidence orbit and progress arc dominate the field;
- grouping: target, remaining, progress and destination are embedded in the field;
- spatial relationship: phases and guardrail are milestones on a path;
- interaction presentation: action and Briefing form a transition dock between trajectory and priorities.

Its grayscale structure is categorically different from A1.

No rejected same-structure recolors are included in the review set.
