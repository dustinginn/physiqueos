# Typography and accessibility

## Weight correction

| Role | Weekly | Midweek | Rule |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Primary weight value | 27 pt | 27 pt | Value and `lb` stay on one line |
| Change/delta | 13 pt | 13 pt | Blue plus signed number; never the dominant value |
| Context/narrative | 15 pt | 15 pt | Shared body scale and line rhythm |
| Section label | 11 pt | 11 pt | Uppercase, cyan evidence accent |

Midweek remains shorter through copy volume and spacing, not by shrinking the metric into a separate miniature style.

## Recovery accessibility

- Weekly and Midweek Sleep graphs expose complete textual summaries with day/value pairs, baseline and status.
- The dashed baseline is named in adjacent text and the accessibility label.
- Green uses marker + text, never color alone.
- All narrative copy is at least 15 pt in the design harness.
- Navigation targets are 44 pt tall.
- Graph labels remain readable and preserve their day/value association under Dynamic Type by keeping the graph atomic and exposing the full VoiceOver summary.
- The disposable harness renders at 402 pt / 3× with a 51 pt top safe-area treatment.
