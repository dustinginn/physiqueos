# Creative-formatting parity proof

The creative pass retains the corrective pass's frozen content contract and strengthens its validator across six renders.

For every F1/F2/F3 dark/mineral-light render, the validator requires:

- all 85 displayed canonical semantics to match the unchanged Build 85 fixture exactly;
- the exact production W-ID sequence after excluding the one authorized `R-FUTURE` insertion;
- identical parent section and source-component mapping;
- the grouped Energy chart in W08 with the exact title, two series, seven dates and fourteen values;
- the same fixture-driven absence of W11, W12, W22, W30, W31 and W32;
- Home and Briefing History navigation labels;
- exactly one Recovery section after Training and before Coach interpretation;
- explicit fixture-only Recovery labeling and no Confidence semantics;
- body/narrative text at least 15 pt in the design harness;
- every Priority Muscle Groups row left-aligned and at least 150 pt wide;
- a chart accessibility label describing seven days;
- three unique grayscale layout signatures.

Result: PASS for F1 dark, F1 mineral light, F2 dark, F2 mineral light, F3 dark and F3 mineral light.

The visual-composition freedom is intentionally not treated as a parity failure. Copy, order, domains, chart semantics and conditional behavior remain hard failures.
