# Weekly creative-formatting directions

All directions use the same frozen renderer, canonical strings, W-ID order, section parents, chart data and Recovery seam. Only visual composition changes.

## F1 — Home-Language Evolution

The closest translation of locked Home into long-form Weekly:

- immersive teal/navy integrated lead with an accurate 68% ring and restrained atmospheric geometry;
- compact strategy strip over the hero field;
- Energy as a warm semantic field with inset metric tiles and a deliberately framed chart;
- mixed containment: Weight and Training are contained, Photos is a smaller inset note, Recovery is a cool field;
- lift highlights use strong accent rails;
- Priority Muscle Groups use full-width, left-aligned inset rows with the exact status sentence intact;
- Coach's Take retains a strong authored navy field.

Grayscale identity: dominant immersive hero, alternating contained/open rhythm, large rounded fields.

## F2 — Modular Briefing

A denser modular system derived from locked Log's Compact Command Center:

- Confidence becomes an inset module within the lead, with the ring visually paired at right;
- strategy facts, Energy metrics and Weight values use compact tiles;
- Energy header is its own module, with chart and metrics nested below without changing their order;
- Training highlights become compact metric modules with delta capsules;
- Priority Muscle Groups use full-width, left-aligned bordered modules—never narrow centered columns;
- Coach actions use compact contained rows.

Grayscale identity: repeated modules, tighter vertical rhythm, visible sectional segmentation.

## F3 — Editorial Premium

An open long-form interpretation of the same product language:

- full-bleed integrated lead with larger headline typography and minimal card chrome;
- sections are separated by strong semantic rules and whitespace instead of repeated containers;
- the production Energy chart remains the visual anchor inside a single contained plotting field;
- Training highlights become editorial evidence rows;
- Priority Muscle Groups become open, full-width, left-aligned rows with exact status copy;
- Recovery is one selective cool field;
- Coach's Take becomes a full-width closing synthesis.

Grayscale identity: open canvas, typographic hierarchy, rule-based rhythm, few strong full-width fields.

The three dark layouts produce distinct measured geometry, card radii, priority-list composition and section heights in `validation.json`; the grayscale-difference gate passes with three unique signatures.
