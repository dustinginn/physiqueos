## What changed since the `2a193138` preview

That preview selected the single top-ranked pattern (the late-week routine change) and let it become the whole briefing. The engine now reasons in four stages:

1. **Every goal-relevant domain is assessed first.** Assessments come from a goal policy resolved from the Goal Contract's canonical evaluation: mode, desiredDirection, and baseline/target values or ranges. The domains are:
   - body trajectory (weight)
   - body composition
   - guardrail
   - training (rhythm and bests)
   - nutrition (with reliability)
   - activity (as a wearable estimate)
   - routine
   - a Recovery/Sleep slot
   - a visual-change (photos) slot
2. **Holistic, goal-relative synthesis.** Complementary insights are chosen by marginal value, with penalties for repeating a domain, a role, or something another insight already covers, and a progress/execution balance. Every omission carries a reason. Standing context (the recent DEXA, a clear guardrail) informs the goal-relative sections without taking recap slots. A limitation is said only when it constrains a claim that would otherwise have been made.
3. **Per-briefing information budgets that change behavior, not word counts:**
   - Midweek (light): a partial window cannot conclude complete-week findings.
   - Weekly (moderate).
   - Monthly (dense): persistent findings are favored over one-off stretches, and each is labeled.
   - DEXA: the outcome leads.
   - Photo: depth scales with visual change, never with a DEXA.
4. **Weekly coach-voice realization.** Each section has one job, and the sections are tested for agreement with each other.
   - Direction words, risks and next steps follow the goal's direction and the scale's actual movement.
   - Exercise bests appear only as recap examples and never explain Goal Confidence.
   - Weight is always considered and is never presented as composition.

Only Weekly is wired to prose now. Midweek, Monthly, DEXA and Photo already run on the same shared picture, synthesis and budgets; their prose is the committed Phase 2/3 work below.
