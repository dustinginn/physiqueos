## 4. What the engine deliberately did *not* say

- **It did not say intake was higher Thursday–Saturday.** Canonical intake for those days (2,415 / 1,922 / 2,915 kcal) doesn't show that. All three days have protein profiles implausible against the Founder's baseline (5–10 robust deviations low), so they are reported as *incomplete logs* and excluded from behavior claims.
- **It attributes no cause.** The shift is `causalClaim: co_occurrence`; it states only what changed together.
- **It gives no Apple Watch numbers,** only directional wording ("well below your usual").
- **It does not treat missing data as behavior.** Absence of training and weigh-ins was claimed only on days the Founder was otherwise observable (activity synced). It was also weighted by the Founder's own weekday routine, so a scheduled rest day would not count.
- **It names no exercise in Goal Confidence.** The explanation is goal-level: DEXA anchor, period evidence, not enough to move the outlook.
- **The recurrence is stated precisely:** "the second multi-day training break in the last five weeks" is backed by the Sep 5–7 training gap, the only other one inside the 28-day lookback.

## 5. Architecture delivered (Phase 1)

- **Shared layer** `src/domain/intelligence/shared/` (`briefing_intelligence_v1`). It runs once on the single V3 `prepare` path that every publisher shares:
  - builds canonical day series from the same nutrition authority the Energy card uses;
  - computes robust personal baselines and weekday-aware habit;
  - detects consecutive-day runs, level shifts, dispersion shifts, frequency changes, observable-day routine gaps, co-occurring routine shifts, and recurrence within the lookback;
  - flags reliability problems (implausible macro profiles, near-duplicate days, partial days), including unreliable *baseline* days;
  - ranks by materiality with restraint gates.
- **Briefing-type policies** (`BriefingIntelligencePolicies.js`). Each type owns its horizon, detectable building blocks and characterizing kinds:
  - Weekly: completed-period recap.
  - Midweek: emerging signals only.
  - Monthly: persistence and change.
  - DEXA/Photo: a 28-day preceding-execution context window.
- **Wiring.** Only **Weekly** is wired in Phase 1. It passes the graduation-overlaid evidence it already read; there is no second repository read.
- **Safety.** The layer is fail-safe: any error falls back to the prior V3 behavior, recorded on the lineage. Without period evidence, V3 runs as before.
- **Consumption.**
  - **Narrative.** The recap leads when the plan holds.
  - **Confidence.** A goal-level explanation rule that applies to every briefing type.
  - **Strategy.** Untouched.
  - **Presentation.** The Weekly presentable uncertainty is bounded to what was surfaced outside Watch or a module (the full profile is kept as `uncertaintyAudit`, and completeness reads the audit). This removes the generic "Still Unresolved" block **server-side, even on the current Native build**.

- **The properties include:**
  - no single-day patterns;
  - absences only on observable days, weighted by weekday routine;
  - unreliable days never read as behavior;
  - causal claims limited to none or co-occurrence;
  - Confidence and recommendation unchanged by characterization;
  - stable weeks keep the concise no-change narrative;
  - no exercise in Goal Confidence;
  - wearable numbers never stated;
  - intake advised against the plan, never against the person's usual;
  - span-accurate wording;
  - the no-evidence path unchanged.

- **The synthetic weeks found real engine weaknesses, which are now fixed:**
  - rest days misread as missed training (fixed with a weekday-aware routine);
  - a single spike becoming a "run" (fixed with a short-run strength rule);
  - weigh-ins alone headlining a week (now supporting-only).
