## Confidence behavior across Goal types and time

Primary confidence means Goal-completion outlook, separate from internal strategy confidence. Direct robust significant outcomes refresh an anchored absolute outlook; a conservative prior does not permanently suppress that view. Directional targets use Goal baseline/progress/remaining requirement and strategy-interval response rate; supported deadline forecasts use remaining time and a discounted rate, not an assumption that one exceptional interval repeats. Untimed Goals receive no manufactured deadline penalty. State/range/duration contracts retain their configured success meaning; immediate successful maintenance is already achieved, whereas a required maintenance duration must be satisfied separately. Multiple objectives combine configured required criteria; guardrails constrain forward completion/actions independently.

The real September fixture remains 79 (anchored outlook approximately 78.54), internal strategy confidence 90. Its 58% completion and 4.2 lb remaining, 49 days available, +5 lb/28-day active-strategy response and clear assessed guardrails earn the forecast, not an instruction to set 79/80. The prior 62 does not materially constrain this decisive repricing. The single-period rate is discounted; emerging-persistence ceiling 88 is not binding. There is room for stronger persistence and completion evidence.

Retained longitudinal tests cover one/multiple supportive weeks, weak proxy limits, one/multiple poor weeks, later decisive repricing, supportive execution plus confirmed persistence, excellent historical findings with deteriorating current execution, no time-only rise and overlapping-window/replay deduplication. Illustrative previous longitudinal trace: supportive 79→79→79→80 (underlying outlook 78.54→78.90→79.39→79.75); poor execution 79→71→63→60 while demonstrated feasibility and historical answers remain intact. These are deterministic test traces, not scheduled weekly awards. Qualified supportive execution updates are bounded to +3 per update, material adverse execution to -8; direct outcomes have a separate repricing policy. Weak proxies cannot create a decisive-outcome-sized jump. No evidence/time alone earns no rise.
